# Phase 2A curated source review

Checked on 2026-09-23. This review concerns a service that fetches feed entries and may retain article titles, short summaries, dates, source attribution, URLs and lexical metadata for recommendations. It does not authorize full article text, images, logos or a third-party feed's wholesale republication. Initial availability checks used public HTTPS endpoints through a web fetcher; that fetcher recognized RSS/XML content types for five candidate URLs but could not expose their XML bodies. A subsequent direct, read-only GET of the NASA news feed resolved its endpoint and channel language. Endpoint availability and individual entry language should be rechecked when ingestion is connected.

The enabled registry is a code-managed admission list keyed by `nasa-recently-published` and the exact `https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/feed/` URL. The key is retained for registry continuity after replacing the original starter-feed endpoint. User-created subscriptions and starter-feed membership do not grant curated status. Its quality score of 85/100 is an editorial judgment based on NASA's institutional provenance, not a measured truth or legal rating. The ingestion task must reject non-English entries before persistence and must not copy images or full article text on the strength of this review. Its safe fetcher must validate any redirect target at ingestion time, including if NASA later changes the endpoint. If entry-level English validation is not implemented, disable this source.
