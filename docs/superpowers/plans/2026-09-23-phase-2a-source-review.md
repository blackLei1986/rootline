# Phase 2A curated source review

Checked on 2026-09-23. This review concerns a service that fetches feed entries and may retain article titles, short summaries, dates, source attribution, URLs and lexical metadata for recommendations. It does not authorize full article text, images, logos or a third-party feed's wholesale republication. Availability checks used the public HTTPS endpoints through a web fetcher; the local shell could not resolve external DNS. The fetcher recognized RSS/XML content types for five URLs but could not expose their XML bodies. Endpoint availability and language should be rechecked when ingestion is connected.

The enabled registry is a code-managed admission list keyed by `nasa-recently-published` and the exact `https://www.nasa.gov/feed/` URL. User-created subscriptions and starter-feed membership do not grant curated status. Its quality score of 85/100 is an editorial judgment based on NASA's institutional provenance, not a measured truth or legal rating. The important operational caveat is that the NASA all-site feed can include Spanish entries and links to third-party material. The ingestion task must reject non-English entries before persistence and must not copy images or full article text on the strength of this review. If entry-level validation is not implemented, disable this source until an English-only endpoint is verified.
